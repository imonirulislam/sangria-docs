Sangria is where your team talks. This page gets you signed in, into a workspace,
and oriented — in a few minutes.

## Sign in

Sign in with the methods your workspace enables:

- **Email + password** — the default for most workspaces.
- **OAuth providers** — sign in with Google, GitHub, or another provider where your
  workspace has it configured.

<Callout title="Which options do I have?">
  The buttons you see on the sign-in screen are the ones your workspace turned on.
  If a provider you expect is missing, ask a workspace admin.
</Callout>

## Join a workspace

You reach a workspace one of two ways:

1. **With a join code** — enter the 6-character code someone shares with you.
2. **With an invite link** — open the link and you'll be taken straight in.

When you join, you're automatically added to the workspace's default channel(s), so
there's always somewhere to start. A personal space — a self-DM, or "notes to self" —
is also ready for you to jot things down.

<Callout type="info" title="No code or link yet?">
  Ask a teammate to share the workspace's join code or an invite link with you. See
  [Workspaces](/docs/help/workspaces) for how those are created and managed.
</Callout>

## Get oriented

The **left sidebar** is home base:

- **Channels** — the shared spaces you belong to.
- **Direct messages** — your 1:1 and group conversations.

Above and around the sidebar you'll find:

- **Activity** — a feed of your notifications: mentions, replies, and reactions.
- **Search** — jump to any message, channel, or person across the workspace.
- **Huddles** — start a quick voice or video call in any channel or DM.
