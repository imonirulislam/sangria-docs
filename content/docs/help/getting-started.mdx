Sangria is where your team talks. This page gets you signed in, into a workspace,
and oriented — in a few minutes.

## Sign in

Sign in with the methods your workspace enables:

- **Email + password** — the default for most workspaces.
- **A social provider** — Google, Microsoft, Apple, GitHub or GitLab, whichever your
  Sangria has been set up with.
- **A passkey** — your fingerprint, face or device PIN, with no password to type.
  See [Passkeys](/docs/help/account-and-security#passkeys).

<Callout title="Which options do I have?">
  The sign-in screen only shows the providers this Sangria is configured for, so you
  won't see a button that can't work. If one you expect is missing, ask whoever runs
  your deployment to add its credentials.
</Callout>

## Confirm your email address

The first time you sign in with an email and password, Sangria emails you a
6-digit code and asks you to type it in. Enter it and you go straight through.

This happens once per account, not every sign-in — including for accounts created
before confirmation was introduced, which is why you may be asked for a code on an
account you've used for a while.

The code is good for 15 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder and
use **Send another** on the code screen. Social sign-ins (Google, Microsoft, and
the rest) skip this step, because the provider has already confirmed your address.

## Using more than one sign-in method

Every sign-in method on the **same email address** lands you in the same account —
it doesn't create a second one — so you can use your password one day and Microsoft
the next. The order you add them in doesn't matter.

Confirming your address is what makes this safe: without proof the address belongs
to you, anyone could reach an account just by claiming its email. So if you sign in
with Google first and add a password later, the two are joined the moment you enter
the emailed code, not before.

## Join a workspace

You reach a workspace one of two ways:

1. **With a join code** — enter the 6-character code someone shares with you.
2. **With an invite link** — open the link and you'll be taken straight in.

When you join, you're automatically added to the workspace's default channel(s), so
there's always somewhere to start. A personal space — a self-DM, or "notes to self" —
is also ready for you to jot things down.

<Callout type="info" title="No code or link yet?">
  Ask a teammate to share the workspace's join code or an invite link with you. See
  [Workspaces](/docs/help/workspaces) for how those are created and managed.
</Callout>

## Get oriented

The **left sidebar** is home base:

- **Channels** — the shared spaces you belong to.
- **Direct messages** — your 1:1 and group conversations.

Above and around the sidebar you'll find:

- **Activity** — a feed of your notifications: mentions, replies, and reactions.
- **Search** — jump to any message, channel, or person across the workspace.
- **Huddles** — start a quick voice or video call in any channel or DM.
