---
title: Huddles
description: Start a quick voice or video call in any channel or DM.
---

A huddle is a quick voice/video call for when a conversation is faster out loud. Start
one right where you're already talking — no scheduling, no links to share.

## Start a huddle

- **In a channel or DM** — click **Huddle** (the headphones button) in the header to
  open it up to everyone there. On a phone the button shows just the headphones icon.
- **From a profile** — start a huddle from someone's profile for a quick 1:1.

Huddles are off in [announcement channels](/docs/help/channels#announcement-channels):
the **Huddle** button is greyed out there for everyone, admins included. A huddle that
was already running when the channel became announcement-only can still be joined until
it ends.

Anyone who can access the channel or DM can **join** an active huddle — while one is
running, the header button changes to **Join Huddle**. Once you're in, it reads **On
Huddle**; click it to leave.

On a computer, the huddle opens in **its own window**. You first see a preview of
yourself, with small bars that move as you speak so you know your mic is working. Pick
your microphone, camera and speaker (press **Test** to hear a chime), then click **Start
huddle** or **Join huddle** and the call carries on in that window, with the huddle's
thread beside it. Your devices are remembered for next time. A
bar at the bottom of the main window lets you mute, bring the huddle window back with
**Show huddle**, or **Leave**. Closing the huddle window asks before it takes you out of
the huddle. If your browser blocks the pop-up, the huddle opens inside the page instead.
On a phone it opens as a sheet over the app.

Huddles running in channels you've joined also show at the bottom of your channel
list, with who's in them and a **Join** button. Huddles in your DMs, and ones
someone invites you to, ring you instead.

## Invite people in

You can **invite specific people** into an active huddle, even if they're not in that
channel. This is handy for pulling in a colleague for a moment without adding them to
the whole channel.

You can only invite people from the same workspace. Guests can't invite anyone into a
channel huddle, and in a DM huddle they can only invite people they share a channel with
(see [Guests](/docs/help/workspaces#guests)).

## During a call

While you're in a huddle you can:

- Switch your **camera**, **microphone** and **speaker** from the arrow next to the
  mic and camera buttons. The speaker option shows only in browsers that can choose
  where sound plays (not Safari).
- **Share your screen**.
- **Invite people** with the button at the bottom left.
- Change the **layout**: a grid, a speaker view, or a sidebar view.

### Change the background

Each huddle has an illustrated scene behind the call, the same for everyone in it. To
pick your own, open **More** (⋯) › **Background** and choose a scene, or choose **Auto**
to go back to the huddle's scene. Your choice only changes what you see, and it's
remembered on that device. If your system is set to reduce motion, the scenes stay still.

<Callout type="info" title="End-to-end encryption">
  Huddles can be end-to-end encrypted.
</Callout>

## After a huddle

When a huddle ends, a message notes who took part. Recent huddles are listed too, so
you can see what you missed and who was on the call.

<Callout title="One huddle at a time">
  A channel or DM has at most one active huddle at a time. If a huddle is already
  running there, you'll join it rather than start a new one.
</Callout>

Huddles are powered by LiveKit.
