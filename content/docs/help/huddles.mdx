---
title: Huddles
description: Start a quick voice or video call in any channel or DM.
---

A huddle is a quick voice/video call for when a conversation is faster out loud. Start
one right where you're already talking — no scheduling, no links to share.

## Start a huddle

- **In a channel or DM** — start a huddle from the header to open it up to everyone
  there.
- **From a profile** — start a huddle from someone's profile for a quick 1:1.

Anyone who can access the channel or DM can **join** an active huddle.

## Invite people in

You can **invite specific people** into an active huddle, even if they're not in that
channel. This is handy for pulling in a colleague for a moment without adding them to
the whole channel.

## During a call

While you're in a huddle you can:

- Switch your **camera** and **microphone**.
- **Share your screen**.

<Callout type="info" title="End-to-end encryption">
  Huddles can be end-to-end encrypted.
</Callout>

## After a huddle

When a huddle ends, a message notes who took part. Recent huddles are listed too, so
you can see what you missed and who was on the call.

<Callout title="One huddle at a time">
  A channel or DM has at most one active huddle at a time. If a huddle is already
  running there, you'll join it rather than start a new one.
</Callout>

Huddles are powered by LiveKit.
