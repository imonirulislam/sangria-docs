---
title: Desktop & mobile apps
description: Run Sangria in your browser, as a desktop or mobile app, or installed as a PWA.
---

Sangria runs wherever you work. Use it in your **browser**, as a **desktop app**, on
**mobile**, or install it as a **PWA** — your workspaces, channels, and messages are
the same everywhere, so you can move between them through the day without losing your
place.

Which one to pick comes down to the machine you're on and what you need from it. The
sections below cover what each option adds.

## Browser

Open Sangria in any modern browser and sign in. There's nothing to install, which
makes it the quickest way to get going — on your own machine, or on a shared or
borrowed one where you'd rather not install anything. When you want native
notifications or huddle screen sharing, move up to the desktop app or install the
PWA.

## Desktop app

The desktop app runs on **macOS, Windows, and Linux**, and it's the fullest way to
use Sangria at your computer. On top of everything the browser does, it adds:

- **Native notifications** in your operating system's notification center, so alerts
  show up alongside the rest of your desktop notifications.
- **Screen sharing** in huddles, for walking through a document or demoing your work.
- **Camera and microphone** for voice and video in [huddles](/docs/help/huddles).

If you spend most of your day in Sangria on a computer, the desktop app is the one
to install.

## Mobile app

The mobile app is available for **iOS and Android**, and adds **push notifications**
so you stay reachable when you're away from your computer. Pair it with the desktop
app to catch mentions on your phone when you've stepped away from your desk — just
remember that notifications are enabled separately on each, as noted below.

## Install as a PWA

You can install Sangria straight from your browser as a Progressive Web App, so it
lives in your dock or app list like a native app without a separate download. Use
your browser's **install prompt** to add it. The installed PWA gives you:

- **App shortcuts** for jumping straight to common places.
- An **offline fallback page** that appears when you're disconnected, so you get a
  clear message instead of a broken tab.

## Push notifications are per-device

Push notifications are **per-device** — each browser or device subscribes
separately. Enabling them on your laptop's browser doesn't cover your phone, and the
mobile app doesn't cover your desktop app; each one has its own subscription.

<Callout type="info" title="Enable notifications on every device">
  Turning on notifications in one place doesn't turn them on everywhere. Enable
  notifications on each browser and device you use, or you'll miss alerts on the
  ones you skipped.
</Callout>

## When your connection drops

If you lose signal or the connection wavers, Sangria shows a small **reconnecting**
banner instead of quietly failing, then slots you back in on its own once you're back
— no reload needed. On the mobile app you can also **pull down from the top** of a
list to force a fresh sync whenever you want one.
