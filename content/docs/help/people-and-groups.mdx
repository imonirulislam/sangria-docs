Two features help you find and reach the right people: the **people directory**
for browsing everyone in the workspace, and **user groups** for mentioning a set
of members at once.

## People directory

The directory is a searchable, paginated list of everyone in your workspace.
Search by name, or filter the list to find who you're after:

- **By role** — owner, admin, or member.
- **By celebration** — who has a birthday or work anniversary this month.
- **By join date** — see who's new, or who's been around longest.

Open a person from the directory to see their [profile](/docs/help/profile-and-status).

## User groups

A user group is a named, **@-mentionable** collection of members. Each group has:

- A **handle** — a unique slug you type to mention the group.
- A **description** — a short line explaining who the group is for.

@mentioning a group in a message notifies its members who are also in that
channel. People in the group who aren't in the channel aren't pulled in.

### Groups and channels

A group can be assigned to one or more **channels**. When it is, anyone added to
the group is **automatically added to those channels** — a quick way to keep a
team in sync as people join.

<Callout title="Who manages groups">
  Admins create user groups and add or remove members. Manage them in
  **Customize workspace → User Groups**.
</Callout>
