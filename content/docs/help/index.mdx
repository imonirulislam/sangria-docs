Welcome to the Sangria Help Center. Sangria is where your team talks: organized
**channels**, one-to-one and group **direct messages**, voice/video **huddles**,
and **integrations** that connect your other tools.

New here? Start with **[Getting started](/docs/help/getting-started)** — it covers
signing in, joining a workspace, and finding your way around.
