title: Messages

Messages are the heart of Sangria. You write them in the **composer** — a rich-text
editor with formatting, **@mentions**, and emoji (including your workspace's custom
emoji) — and send them to a channel or a direct message.

## Format and send

Type in the composer at the bottom of any channel or DM, then send. Along the way you
can:

- Apply **formatting** as you type.
- Add **@mentions** to pull people in (see [Mentions](#mentions)).
- Drop in emoji and **custom emoji**.
- Attach one or more **files** (see [Files & attachments](#files-attachments)).

## Formatting

Toggle the formatting bar with the **Aa** button below the composer. The editor
supports:

- **Bold**, *italic*, underline, and strikethrough
- **Links** — select text, then add a URL
- **Ordered** and **bulleted** lists
- **Blockquotes** for quoting text
- **Inline code** and **code blocks** (see below)

### Code: inline vs. block

There are two separate ways to mark up code:

- **Inline code** wraps a short snippet inside a line — a function name, value, or
  path like `getBlockedCategories()` — in a small monospace chip.
- A **code block** is a multi-line box for a whole snippet, with monospace text and
  horizontal scrolling for long lines. Reach for it when you're pasting more than a
  word or two.

Both look the same in the sent message as they do while you're typing.

## Edit and delete

- **Edit your own messages.** Your workspace may set an **edit window** (in minutes)
  after which a message can no longer be changed; a value of `0` means there's no
  limit.
- **Delete your own messages**, where your workspace's policy allows it.
- **Admins** can edit or delete any message.

<Callout type="info" title="Admin edits are logged">
  When an admin edits someone else's message, the change is recorded in the workspace
  audit log.
</Callout>

## Reactions

React to any message with an emoji or a custom emoji. Click a reaction again to remove
it. Each person can add a given emoji to a message once.

## Mentions

Mentions notify the people you name:

- **@person** — notifies that one person.
- **@user-group** — notifies the group's members who are in that channel.
- **@channel**, **@everyone**, and **@here** — broadcasts that notify people in the
  channel.

## Threads

Reply to a message to start a **thread**. Replies are grouped under the parent message,
so a side conversation stays out of the main flow while everyone can still follow it.

## Forward a message

Forward a message to another channel or DM to share it where it's more useful. You can
only forward a message you're able to read in the first place.

## Schedule a message

Compose a message now and **schedule** it to send later. You can cancel a scheduled
message any time before it sends.

## Pin a message

**Pin** an important message in a channel or DM to keep it handy. Pinned messages
appear in the **Pins** tab.

## Save for later and reminders

Save any message to your personal **Later** list to come back to it. Later is organized
into **In progress**, **Completed**, and **Archived**. You can also set a **reminder
time** so a saved message resurfaces when you need it.

When a reminder comes due, **Sangriabot** — a permanent workspace assistant present in
every workspace — sends it to you as a direct message, with a link back to the original
item. It's the same DM Sangriabot uses to welcome you when you first join a workspace.
That DM follows your regular notification settings (mute it like any other DM if you'd
rather not be pushed), and it can't be uninstalled or removed.

## Polls

Type **`/poll`** (optionally followed by your question, e.g. `/poll What should we
order?`) in a channel or DM composer to open the poll creator. Add up to 10 options,
choose whether people can pick **more than one** answer, and whether voting is
**anonymous** (nobody, including you, sees who picked what — only the counts).

In a channel, you can also make the poll's **creator anonymous** — Sangriabot posts
the poll instead of you, so the channel never learns who asked. You (and admins) can
still close and edit it; the option isn't available for DM polls, since there's no
one to stay anonymous from in a 1:1 conversation.

Two more ways to shape a poll:
- **Hide results until closed** — voting is "blind": nobody but the creator (or an
  admin) sees live counts while the poll is open. Everyone sees full results once it
  closes.
- **Auto-close** — pick a time (a preset like "In 1 hour" or a custom date) and the
  poll closes itself, no need to remember to close it manually.

Once posted, click an option to vote — results update live for everyone as votes come
in (unless results are hidden). Picking a different option in a single-choice poll
swaps your vote; clicking your current pick again removes it. The person who created
the poll (or a workspace admin) can **close** it at any time, which stops new votes
and reveals a final, ranked results view — or **edit** it beforehand: change the
question, rename an option, or add new options. An option that already has votes
can't be removed, to avoid losing anyone's answer.

## Files & attachments

Attach one or more files to a message. Everything shared in a channel is indexed in its
**Files & links** tab, so you can find a file or link long after it scrolled away.

Click an **image, video, or PDF** to open it in a full-size preview instead of
downloading it. Any other file type downloads directly when clicked — hover a file to
reveal a dedicated download button either way.

## Link previews

Links you post **auto-unfurl** into a preview — title, description, and image — so
people can see what you shared without leaving the conversation. The author of a message
can hide a specific preview.

## Custom emoji

Upload **custom emoji** for your workspace, then use them in messages and reactions just
like standard emoji.
