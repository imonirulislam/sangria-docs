---
title: Messages
description: Format, edit, react, thread, mention, pin, schedule, save, and share messages.
---

Messages are the heart of Sangria. You write them in the **composer** — a rich-text
editor with formatting, **@mentions**, and emoji (including your workspace's custom
emoji) — and send them to a channel or a direct message.

## Format and send

Type in the composer at the bottom of any channel or DM, then send. Along the way you
can:

- Apply **formatting** as you type.
- Add **@mentions** to pull people in (see [Mentions](#mentions)).
- Drop in emoji and **custom emoji**.
- Attach one or more **files** (see [Files & attachments](#files-attachments)).

## Edit and delete

- **Edit your own messages.** Your workspace may set an **edit window** (in minutes)
  after which a message can no longer be changed; a value of `0` means there's no
  limit.
- **Delete your own messages**, where your workspace's policy allows it.
- **Admins** can edit or delete any message.

<Callout type="info" title="Admin edits are logged">
  When an admin edits someone else's message, the change is recorded in the workspace
  audit log.
</Callout>

## Reactions

React to any message with an emoji or a custom emoji. Click a reaction again to remove
it. Each person can add a given emoji to a message once.

## Mentions

Mentions notify the people you name:

- **@person** — notifies that one person.
- **@user-group** — notifies the group's members who are in that channel.
- **@channel**, **@everyone**, and **@here** — broadcasts that notify people in the
  channel.

## Threads

Reply to a message to start a **thread**. Replies are grouped under the parent message,
so a side conversation stays out of the main flow while everyone can still follow it.

## Forward a message

Forward a message to another channel or DM to share it where it's more useful. You can
only forward a message you're able to read in the first place.

## Schedule a message

Compose a message now and **schedule** it to send later. You can cancel a scheduled
message any time before it sends.

## Pin a message

**Pin** an important message in a channel or DM to keep it handy. Pinned messages
appear in the **Pins** tab.

## Save for later and reminders

Save any message to your personal **Later** list to come back to it. Later is organized
into **In progress**, **Completed**, and **Archived**. You can also set a **reminder
time** so a saved message resurfaces when you need it.

## Files & attachments

Attach one or more files to a message. Everything shared in a channel is indexed in its
**Files & links** tab, so you can find a file or link long after it scrolled away.

## Link previews

Links you post **auto-unfurl** into a preview — title, description, and image — so
people can see what you shared without leaving the conversation. The author of a message
can hide a specific preview.

## Custom emoji

Upload **custom emoji** for your workspace, then use them in messages and reactions just
like standard emoji.
