---
title: Profile & status
description: Fill in your profile, set your presence, and share a custom status.
---

Your profile tells teammates who you are, and your status tells them what you're
up to right now. Both are edited from your profile settings.

## Your profile

Fill in as much or as little as you like:

- **Name**, **title**, and **department**
- **Pronouns** and **name pronunciation**
- **Timezone** and **location**
- **Birthday** and **phone**
- A **profile photo**

Teammates see these fields when they open your profile from a message or the
[people directory](/docs/help/people-and-groups).

## Presence

Presence shows whether you're reachable, and it's set **automatically** from your
activity:

- **Online** — you're active.
- **Away** — you've gone idle; Sangria switches you to away for you.
- **Offline** — you're not connected.

## Custom status

Set a short status with an emoji — like a coffee cup for "back in 10" — and choose
when it should **auto-clear**:

- 30 minutes
- 1 hour
- 4 hours
- Today
- This week
- Don't clear — it stays until you clear it yourself

## Timezone

Your timezone is **auto-detected** when you sign in, and you can edit it in
settings. Sangria uses it to:

- Show teammates your **local time**, so they know when it's a good time to reach
  you.
- Evaluate your notification **quiet hours** in your own time.

<Callout title="Keep your timezone right">
  If you travel or move, update your timezone in settings — otherwise your local
  time and quiet hours will be off. See [Notifications](/docs/help/notifications)
  for how quiet hours work.
</Callout>
