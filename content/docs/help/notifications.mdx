---
title: Notifications
description: Control what you're notified about, and when.
---

Notifications keep you on top of what matters without drowning you in the rest. Tune
them at three levels: your **activity feed**, each **channel**, and your **global
preferences**.

## Activity feed

Your activity feed is a running list of what's relevant to you. Filter it by:

- **All**
- **Direct messages**
- **Mentions**
- **Threads**

Mark items as read, or clear them once you've dealt with them.

## Per-channel notifications

Set each channel to notify you on:

- **All** messages
- **Mentions** only
- **Nothing**

You can also **mute** a channel, optionally until a time you choose. See
[Channels](/docs/help/channels).

## Global preferences

Open your account **preferences** to control notifications everywhere:

- Turn notifications **on or off**.
- Toggle **sound** and **vibration**.
- Enable **desktop push** and **mobile push**.
- Set **quiet hours** — a start/end window during which no push is sent. Quiet hours
  use your timezone.
- Toggle notification **categories**: messages, mentions, reactions, channel updates,
  and workspace updates.

<Callout type="info" title="Quiet hours follow your timezone">
  Quiet hours are based on the timezone set on your profile. Keep it accurate so the
  window lands where you expect. See [Profile & status](/docs/help/profile-and-status).
</Callout>

## Pause notifications

Need quiet for a bit? From the account menu (your avatar), choose **Pause
notifications** and pick how long — **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **2 hours**, or
**until tomorrow**. Resume any time from the same place.

While paused, Sangria **silences the alert** — no push, no banner — but still records
mentions and messages in your **Activity feed**, so nothing is lost: you catch up the
moment you resume. Teammates can see you're paused, too — a small slash appears on
your [presence dot](/docs/help/profile-and-status), so they know you may not respond
right away.

<Callout type="info" title="Pause vs. quiet hours">
  **Quiet hours** are a recurring daily window. **Pause** is a one-off snooze for the
  next 30 minutes to tomorrow. Use pause when you need to focus now; use quiet hours
  for your regular off-hours.
</Callout>

## Highlight words

Add **highlight words** — keywords that notify you like a mention whenever they appear.
Matching is case-insensitive, and you can add up to **50** words.

## Push across your devices

Notifications can arrive as push on the web, desktop, iOS, Android, and installed PWAs.
Each device subscribes separately, so you'll get push on the ones you're set up on.
