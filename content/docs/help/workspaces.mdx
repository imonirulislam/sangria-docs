title: Workspaces

A workspace is an isolated space with its own members, channels, direct messages,
and integrations. Nothing in one workspace is visible from another.

## Create or join a workspace

- **Create a workspace** — set it up and a unique join code is generated for you to
  share.
- **Join an existing workspace** — use its **6-character join code** (case-insensitive)
  or an invite link someone shares with you.

<Callout type="info" title="Why joining is rate-limited">
  Joining by code is limited to a handful of attempts per 10 minutes, so codes can't
  be guessed by brute force. If you hit the limit, wait a few minutes and try again.
</Callout>

## Invite people

Open the workspace menu and choose **Invite people**.

- **Admins and owners** can send an email invitation from the **Email** tab. Pick **Member** or **Guest**, and
  optionally the channels to add them to. Members also join the workspace's auto-join
  channels; guests join only the channels you pick. The link in the email expires
  after 7 days.
- **Everyone** can share the workspace's join code or copy its invite link (for admins,
  it's on the **Join code** tab, along with **Reset code**).

If a picked channel is archived, or you lose access to it before the invite is
accepted, the person simply isn't added to that channel. Sending, revoking and
accepting invitations all appear in the workspace's **Audit log**. Pending invitations
can be resent or revoked under **Customize workspace → Invitations**.

## Workspace settings

Admins and owners manage the workspace under **Customize workspace → Settings**:

- **Name, description, and icon** — edit how the workspace presents itself. The name
  can be up to 80 characters and can't contain a link (it appears in invitation
  emails); the description can be up to 500.
- **Reset the join code** — generate a fresh code. Existing members keep their access;
  only the old code stops working.

## Ownership

Ownership has two levels:

- **Primary owner** — the person who created the workspace. There is exactly one.
- **Owners** — any number of additional owners with owner-level control.

Only the **primary owner** can transfer primary ownership (to another owner) or delete
the workspace.

## Permissions & policies

Admins can tighten some capabilities from "everyone" to "admins only". These policies
include:

- **Who can create channels.**
- **The message edit window** — how long after posting a message can still be edited.
- **Whether members can delete their own messages.**
- **Who can use @channel, @everyone and @here** — everyone, members but not guests,
  or only admins. Apps can always use them.
- **Require two-factor authentication** — when on, every member must set up 2FA
  before they can use the workspace. New workspaces have this on by default. See
  [Account & security](/docs/help/account-and-security#two-factor-authentication).

<Callout title="Where policies live">
  Configure these under **Customize workspace** (admin/owner only). Changing a policy
  affects everyone in the workspace.
</Callout>

## Members

When you remove a member, they are **deactivated** rather than erased: they lose access
to the workspace, but their past messages stay attributed to them so conversations
still make sense. An admin can **reactivate** a deactivated member later to restore
their access.

## Guests

A **guest** is someone from outside your team (a client, contractor or partner) who
joins with limited access.

- **Only admins can invite guests.** An admin sends an email invite with the **guest**
  role, picking the channels the guest should start in, or changes an existing
  member's role to guest.
- **Guests only see the channels they've been added to.** They aren't added to
  auto-join channels, they don't get access to public channels automatically, and
  they can't join channels themselves.
- **Any member can add a guest to a public channel**, as can admins. Private channels
  work as usual: only people already in the channel, or a workspace admin, can add
  someone.
- **Guests can't add people.** They can't add anyone to a channel or use `/invite`.
- **Guests see a smaller directory.** They can only find, mention and message the people
  they share a channel with, and open the profiles of people they share a channel or DM
  with.
- **Guests don't see contact details.** Other people's email addresses and phone numbers
  are hidden from guests.
- **In huddles,** a guest can't invite people into a channel huddle. In a DM huddle they
  can only invite people they share a channel with.
