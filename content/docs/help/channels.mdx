---
title: Channels
description: Create channels, organize your sidebar, and manage who's in them.
---

Channels are shared spaces for a topic, project, or team. Anyone in a **public**
channel can read and post; **private** channels are invite-only. Every workspace
has at least one default channel that everyone joins automatically.

## Create a channel

1. Click the **+** next to **Channels** in the sidebar (or use the create button in
   the channel browser).
2. **Step 1 — Name it.** Channel names are lowercased and use hyphens instead of
   spaces (`marketing-campaigns`, not `Marketing Campaigns`). If your workspace has
   [naming prefixes](#naming-prefixes), pick one here.
3. **Step 2 — Describe it.** Add an optional description and choose **Public** or
   **Private**. Create the channel.

<Callout title="Public vs private">
  You can make a channel private at creation. The workspace's default channel can't
  be made private — it's the one space everyone shares.
</Callout>

## Topic vs description

Channels have two separate fields:

- **Topic** — a short, at-a-glance line shown in the header (e.g. "Q3 launch — ship
  Aug 1"). Meant to change often.
- **Description** — a longer explanation of what the channel is for, shown in the
  channel's details.

Both are edited from the channel header or the channel's details panel by a channel
admin or a workspace admin.

## Organize your sidebar

Your sidebar is yours — how you arrange it doesn't affect anyone else.

- **Sections** — group channels and DMs under custom, collapsible headings (with an
  emoji). Create a section, then drag items into it.
- **Drag and drop** — reorder channels and move them between sections.
- **Collapse** — collapse a section to hide its contents until you need them.

## Membership & notifications

- **Join a public channel** — open it from the channel browser and join; you can
  leave anytime.
- **Private channels** — an existing member or a workspace admin adds you.
- **Per-channel notifications** — set each channel to notify you on **All** messages,
  **Mentions** only, or **Nothing**. You can also **mute** a channel (optionally
  until a specific time). See [Notifications](/docs/help/notifications).

## Naming prefixes

Admins can define a list of allowed **prefixes** (like `proj-`, `team-`, `help-`),
each with a short description, plus an optional maximum name length. When prefixes
are set, the create dialog guides everyone toward consistent names. Manage these in
**Customize workspace → Channels**.

## Channel admins

A channel can have its own admins (separate from workspace admins). A channel admin
can edit the channel's topic/description, manage its membership, and pin or remove
messages in it — without being a workspace-wide admin.

## Delete a channel

A workspace admin or channel admin can delete a channel from its management page.
The default channel can't be deleted. Deleting removes the channel and its messages
for everyone.
