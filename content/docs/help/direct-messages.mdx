title: Direct Messages

---

Direct messages are private conversations, separate from channels. A DM is either
**1:1** with one person or a **group DM** with three or more people.

## Start a conversation

- **1:1** — pick a person to open (or reopen) a private conversation with them.
- **Group DM** — pick three or more people to chat together.

<Callout type="info" title="No duplicate groups">
  If you open a group DM with the exact same set of people again, Sangria reuses the
  existing conversation instead of creating a duplicate.
</Callout>

## Name and topic

Any member of a group can give it a bit of identity:

- **Name** — a custom label so the group is easy to recognize in your sidebar.
- **Topic** — a short line describing what the conversation is about.

## Manage the conversation

Open a conversation's **details** to see and edit its:

- **Name** and **topic**.
- **Member list** — including **adding people**.

<Callout title="Adding people to a 1:1">
  Adding someone to a 1:1 creates a new group DM with everyone in it — the original
  1:1 stays as it was.
</Callout>

## Mute a conversation

If a DM is noisy, **mute** it and it stops notifying you. You'll still see it in your
sidebar and can open it anytime. See [Notifications](/docs/help/notifications) for how
muting fits with your other notification settings.

## Good to know

DMs always live within a single workspace. You can't direct-message someone who's in a
different workspace.
